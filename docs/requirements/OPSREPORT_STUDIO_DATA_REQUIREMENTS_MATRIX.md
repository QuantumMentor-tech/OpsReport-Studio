## 1. Matrix Overview & Controlled Vocabulary

This matrix cross-references all data fields and operational concepts against the seven approved report categories of OpsReport Studio.

### Controlled Value Legend:

- **CONFIRMED:** Formally established by explicit user mandate (Step 1 Baseline Section 3.1).
- **OBSERVED:** Directly verified through inspection of an official customer master template. _(Note: Classified as Pending Template Inspection where files are not yet supplied)_.
- **CANDIDATE:** Strongly recommended based on control-room operational research, but subject to final template verification.
- **NOT APPLICABLE:** The field has no logical or operational relevance to this report category.
- **UNKNOWN:** Status cannot be determined until customer master templates or domain supervisors provide clarification.

## 3. Key Observations & Invariants

1. **Universal Station Coupling:** Every single report category requires a station reference. However, the operational multiplicity differs sharply:
   - `RPT-INCIDENT` couples to a **single station reference** (identifying the incident venue).
   - The remaining six inspection categories (`RPT-STATION-LIGHT`, `RPT-FAN`, `RPT-POLE-LIGHT`, `RPT-FIRE-EXT`, `RPT-PILLAR`, `RPT-FENCE`) require the **complete ordered station sequence** to structure their audit rows.
2. **Core Metadata Consistency:** All reports share `report_date`, `inspector_name`, `station_name`, and `general_remarks`. This consistency enables a modular form header system across the application.
3. **Pending Physical Verification:** When physical customer template files are provided, this matrix will be promoted from `CANDIDATE` to `OBSERVED` or `CONFIRMED`.
